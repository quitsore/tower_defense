# TowerDefense

## Game goal
Defeat every monster and let your base survive

## Rules
Only a given amount of max. towers on the map. You can't buy towers while fighting. Monsters are coming as waves. Before the next wave you can bye the towers. The shopping time is limited. If you are dragging a tower at the moment when the wave started, you can still finish the purchase.

## Characters/Monsters
Goblins, Orcs (very tanky, slower and more damage), Spiders (very fast movement)

## Weapons/Towers
Stone tower, Meteor tower (less damage, less range, but faster shot reload)
