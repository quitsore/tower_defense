# TowerDefense

## Game goal
Defeat every monster and let your base survive

## Rules
Only a given amount of max. towers on the map. You can't buy towers before 
fighting. Monsters are coming as waves. Before the next wave you can bye 
the towers. The shopping time is limited. The remaining time for shopping
you can see on the wheel clock in the right bottom corner. After the time
has elapsed, no shopping is possible, and the monsters attack.
Just click on a tower on the right panel and move it to a free placeholder.
If you are dragging a tower at the moment when the wave started, you can 
still finish the purchase.

## Characters/Monsters
Goblins, Orcs (very tanky, slower and more damage), Spiders (very fast movement)

## Weapons/Towers
Stone tower, Meteor tower (less damage, less range, but faster shot reload)
